## What is on the list

Every company on the list:

- hires its developers directly (no agencies or recruiters),
- has posted a remote PHP role in the last 12 months (Laravel, Symfony, WordPress and other PHP frameworks count),
- links to its own careers page.

## How to use it

Open **[rasoulrahimii.github.io/PHP-Remote-Jobs](https://rasoulrahimii.github.io/PHP-Remote-Jobs/)**.

- **Remote region**: show only companies that hire where you live.
- **Hiring now**: show only companies with an open PHP role.
- **Search**: find a company, industry or benefit. Press <kbd>/</kbd> to jump to the search box.
- **Open roles →**: go to the company's careers page.
- **Share**: your filters are saved in the page address, so you can bookmark or share a search.

Each card shows when the company was last checked. **Hiring** means a remote PHP role was open at that time. **No active openings** means the company hired for PHP in the last 12 months but has nothing open now. Always check the careers page before you apply.

## Add a company

Check the [rules for adding a company](CONTRIBUTING.md#inclusion-criteria), then [suggest it in an issue](https://github.com/rasoulrahimii/PHP-Remote-Jobs/issues/new?template=suggest-company.yml) or add it to [`companies.yml`](companies.yml) in a pull request. Found a broken link or wrong information? [Report it](https://github.com/rasoulrahimii/PHP-Remote-Jobs/issues/new?template=report-problem.yml).
